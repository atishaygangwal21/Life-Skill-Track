# Grit and Growth Mindset


## Grit
### Q1. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines.
This video teaches us that success is not limited to talent or intelligence. But, it is about having grit, which means that staying passionate, committed, and persistent towards long term goals. 
"Keep working toward your goal, learn from failures, and don't give up easily."

## Introduction to Growth Mindset
### Q2. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines
The belief that intelligence and abilities can be developed through effort, learning, and persistence rather than being fixed traits. It encourages seeing challenges and failures as opportunities to improve and grow.
## Understanding Internal Locus of Control

### Q3. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?
It means believing that the results in your life are influenced by factors you can control, such as your own hard work, effort, and actions.The video explains that having an internal locus of control is important for staying motivated. When you believe that your actions can change your situation, you are more likely to keep trying instead of blaming external factors.

## How to build a Growth Mindset
### Q4. What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset?
    1.Believe in your ability to figure things out.
    2.Honor the struggle.
    3.See failures and difficulties as part of the learning process.
    4.Focus on effort and improvement.
    5.Keep learning, growing, and challenging yourself.

## Mindset
### Q5 What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?
    1.Take 100% responsibility for my learning.
    2.Stay with a problem until I solve it instead of quitting.
    3.Put in more effort to improve my understanding.
    4.Never write code that I don’t understand.
    5.Treat confusion, discomfort, and errors as learning opportunities.
    6.Use documentation, Google, Stack Overflow, GitHub Issues, and the Internet before asking for help.
    7.Stay relaxed, focused, and confident when facing new challenges.
    8.Take ownership of my projects and their completion.
    9.Focus on mastery by continuously practising and improving.
    10.Complete my code by making it work, readable, modular, and efficient.
