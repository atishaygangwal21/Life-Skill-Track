# Learning Process

In this document, I have answered the questions related to the **Learning Process** assignment in Life-Skills-Track.

## 1. How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

### Q1 - What is the Feynman Technique?

**Answer:**  
The **Feynman Technique** is a way to deeply understand and learn any topic. It states that one of the best ways to learn a topic is to try to **explain it to others in simple words**.

## 2. Learning How to Learn TED Talk by Barbara Oakley

### Q2 - In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

**Answer:**  
The most interesting idea for me in this video was when **Barbara Oakley** talked about a technique associated with **Thomas Edison**. I found it fascinating because it showed how we can move between **focused mode and diffuse mode** while learning or solving problems.

### Thomas Edison's Technique

Edison reportedly used this technique:

- He would sit comfortably in a chair while thinking about a problem.
- He would hold a few ball bearings in his hand.
- He would relax and let his mind wander while thinking loosely about the problem.
- As he started falling asleep, the ball bearings would drop.
- The sound would wake him up before he entered deep sleep.
- He would immediately write down the ideas or connections that came to his mind.
- He would then return to focused thinking and work on those ideas.

### Practical Lesson

This taught me that we should not try to study or solve problems with **100% concentration all the time**.

Instead, we can follow this cycle:

**Focus → Get Stuck → Take a Break → Let Your Mind Wander → Come Back → Focus Again**

We don't need to use Edison's ball-bearing technique. Simple activities like **going for a walk, taking a shower, exercising, sitting quietly, or taking a short break** can give our brain time to relax and make new connections.

### Q3 - What are active and diffuse modes of thinking?

**Answer:**

- **Active Mode:** Active mode is when we concentrate fully on one specific task or problem.

- **Diffuse Mode:** Diffuse mode is when we relax and stop consciously focusing on the problem. This gives our brain space to make connections between different ideas.

## 3. Learn Anything in 20 Hours

### Q4 - According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

**Answer:**  
According to the video, the following steps should be taken when approaching a new topic:

1. **Deconstruct the skill.**
2. **Learn enough to self-correct.**
3. **Remove barriers to practice.**
4. **Practice for at least 20 hours.**

## 4. Learning Principles in the Bootcamp

### What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

**Answer:**  
I will try to implement these learnings going forward:

- Use the **Feynman Technique** to explain concepts in my own words.
- Balance **focused mode and diffuse mode** while learning.
- Break new topics into smaller, manageable parts and practice them consistently.
- Focus on **understanding and implementing concepts**, rather than just memorizing them.
- Remove distractions like my phone and social media while working.
- Practice with **100% involvement and concentration**.
- Make learning a habit and push through the initial frustration when learning something new.
- Teach others what I learn to check whether I truly understand the concept.
- Track my time and make sure I complete my practice and assignments within the given deadlines.
- Be curious, make learning enjoyable, and take **ownership of my learning process**.
